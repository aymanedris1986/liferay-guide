"Web Content or Object?" — the tie-breakers

Most arguments on a project are about this one boundary. When the first table doesn't settle it, ask these in order. The first "yes" wins:

#QuestionYes →
1Is it submitted by site visitors or by another system rather than written by staff?Object
2Must editors preview it, schedule it, and roll back to last week's version?Web Content
3Does anything need to count, sum, or filter it by typed values (dates, numbers, status)?Object
4Does one record point at another record that has its own life (employee → department)?Object relationship
5Is the main value prose with images inside it?Web Content
Both are fine to display

This decision rarely depends on the front end. Collections, display page templates, and fragment mapping work for both articles and object entries. Decide based on how the data is owned and changed, not on how it will be shown.

Most "we need a customisation" requests are solved one or two rungs to the left of where the team starts. Ask what the cheapest rung that satisfies the requirement is, then stop there.
